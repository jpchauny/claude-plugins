# Glossary

The shared vocabulary of the spec-driven design process. One term, one
meaning; a word used here with another meaning is a mistake. Terms in
alphabetical order. The Spanish word in parentheses is the one used in
conversation.

## Facet (faceta)

One property of a specification that its author decides on or checks while
writing it: for example its completeness, its verifiability, or how much it
prescribes the design. A facet is examined through exactly one lens. A facet
names what to look at, not what is required: what is required for a facet is
stated by a rule.

## Lens (lente)

One of the angles from which a specification is examined: who reads it, what
it says, how it says it, how it is traced and changes, and how it is worked
on. A lens groups facets; every facet belongs to exactly one lens.
